- implementation plans may refine **how** a REQ will be satisfied but may not weaken **what** it requires;
- tasks may decompose a REQ but may not silently merge independent obligations back together;
- code/tests that reveal a genuine specification defect trigger a versioned amendment rather than an undocumented reinterpretation;
- new product functionality requires a separate specification path and remains outside the current remediation milestone.

The next stage is planning, not implementation-by-impulse.
